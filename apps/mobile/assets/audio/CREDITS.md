# Demo audio credits

These recordings are used as **demo audio** for two catalog tracks so the Mmusic player can be tested with real playback. They are not the catalog recordings and are labelled "Demo audio" in the app (Now Playing → "Demo audio" → credits sheet).

Files are unmodified originals downloaded on 2026-10-08 from the author's site. Size and MP3/ID3 tags (title, artist, ISRC) were checked after download.
